⚠ Safety Notice

Start at low volume and increase gradually. Stop immediately if the sound distorts or the phone feels warm near the speaker.

This tool won't fix a genuinely damaged driver. If the problem persists after a few runs, your speaker likely needs proper cleaning or professional repair — not more sweeps.

iPhone users: Make sure the silent mode switch (left side of phone) is OFF (no orange showing), and turn up your media volume.
